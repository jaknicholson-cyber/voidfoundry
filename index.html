KNOW YOUR CANON

LORE PROTOCOL

The Foundry is designed to respect the shape of the setting without pretending its invented history is official.

◆ CANON ANCHOR

Established background

Gene-seed parentage, known flaws, Codex organisation and accepted Founding terminology inform the generator.

◆ ORIGINAL FAN LORE

Made for your Chapter

Chapter name, heraldry, fortress, world, fauna, customs, characters and battles are invented for your dossier.

◆ SPECULATIVE

Explicit exceptions

Unrecorded genealogy and original Second Founding successors are flagged as speculative. AI-enhanced records can still be wrong and should be reviewed against published lore.

◆ IMAGE GENERATION

Optional connected AI

Without a server API key, the Foundry uses original procedural world landscapes and armour illustrations. With an API key it can create new concept art for every record.

For publication, avoid official artwork, copied codex passages and false claims of Games Workshop endorsement. Review their fan-content and IP guidance before making a public website.
